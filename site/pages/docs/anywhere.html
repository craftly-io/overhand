Free: your phone over Tailscale.

  • Your own network Settings → Phone serves {{name}} on your Tailscale network with tailscale serve, so only your own devices reach it.
  • Pair the phone Scan the QR code or type the one-time code, valid for 10 minutes. The computer's own browser needs no pairing: overhand open opens it signed in.
  • Push Set up an ntfy topic (its URL in your keychain, its name under Settings → Integrations) and questions and alerts reach your phone. When your phone can reach {{name}}, a lead's question carries answer buttons that work once.

Pro and Team: from any device.

  • No network setup Open {{relayHost}} on any device and sign in. Your browser connects straight to your computer; when the computer is off, so are your agents.
  • Push from your computer Your computer encrypts each notification and sends it through your browser's own push service.
  • The plan Access from anywhere and push come with Pro and Team. Everything on your computer works without them.

How linking works.

  • Signed up first? Until a computer is linked, {{relayHost}} shows the install line first, then how to link, and says {{w.homeLinked}} by itself once your computer is. Linking needs no plan; reaching it from anywhere does.
  • 1. Set it up Settings → Phone → Access from anywhere → {{w.anywhereSetUp}}. Your computer makes a key of its own and opens {{relayHost}} in your browser.
  • 2. Confirm it's yours Sign in, then check the computer's name and key fingerprint. The relay signs a link certificate for that key and hands it back to your computer.
  • 3. Only its own Your computer takes the certificate only for its own key and its own one-time request.
  • 4. Kept on While your plan is active, your computer renews its access every few hours, and a short outage at the relay doesn't turn hosted features off.
  • What the relay sees It introduces your browser to your computer and never carries the traffic. How the connection is secured.

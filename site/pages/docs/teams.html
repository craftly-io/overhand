Set up a team.

  • Each person runs their own Everyone on the team runs {{name}} on their own computer, linked as on Access from anywhere.
  • Settings → You → Team Create the team, invite people with a link, give roles, set each project's owner and backup, see your seats, and choose whether your results share pull request links.
  • Roles The owner holds the {{teamPlan}} plan. Admins invite and remove people and set project owners. Members see the team, answer, claim, comment and hand off.
  • The plan {{teamPlan}} is priced per seat; see Pricing.

Working side by side.

  • Me and Team Now gets a switch between your own view and the team's, where each person has their own rings and only what waits on you is orange.
  • Follow someone Click a face in the header.
  • Claim it {{w.teamOnIt}} says you've got an item, so nobody does it twice.
  • Hand it off Drag an item onto a teammate, or use {{w.teamHandOff}}. It waits with their agents until they give it to one.
  • Mention @name in a comment notifies that one person.

Questions find their person.

  • The project's owner first An agent's question goes to its project's owner. After {{questionFallback}} minutes without an answer it goes to the backup too.
  • Only metadata The team sees status, questions, results and hand-offs, never code, diffs, keys or transcripts. What the relay keeps on Team.
  • In depth How teams work, what is shared, security and cost: docs/teams.md.

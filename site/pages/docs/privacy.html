What stays.

  • Your code and your agents' work Repos, diffs, transcripts and logs stay on your computer, under ~/.overhand and your own folders.
  • Your keys In your secret store, or the file you opted in to. Where things run.

What leaves, and where to.

  • To your model What your agents send to the model provider you chose, just as they do without {{name}}. A local model's prompts stay on your computer.
  • To GitHub and your tools Through your own sign-ins: gh for pull requests and issues, and any tracker, error service or notification you connect, such as ntfy or a status update's webhook.
  • A release check Once a day {{name}} asks GitHub whether a newer release is out.
  • On Pro and Team Your sign-in, your computer's link and your plan go to the relay at {{relayHost}}; on Team, the team's metadata too. What the relay does and doesn't see.
  • On this website Cookieless page counts from Cloudflare Web Analytics, with no personal data.

What the hosted services keep, and your rights, are in the privacy policy.

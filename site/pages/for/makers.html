Ideas to pull requests, without living in a terminal.

Say what you want in your own words, like "make the invite page friendlier". The agent writes a plan in plain words first and builds nothing until you say go ahead.

"I don't read diffs."

Review the change on its preview instead. Pin a note on the page, and it goes to the same agent as its next fix round.

"I'm not sure what's inside the app."

Start with a read-only check-up. It lists what's worth fixing in plain words, and any finding goes to an agent in one click.

"It asks me things I can't answer."

Most questions come with the answer the agent recommends and what happens if you're busy. A small "?" beside a word says what it means here.
